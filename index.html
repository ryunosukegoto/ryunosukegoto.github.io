I am a PhD Candidate at Stanford’s Biomedical Data Science program and a Knight-Hennessy Scholar. I am advised by Prof. Jonathan Pritchard and Prof. Robert Tibshirani. Previously, I was a Chief Resident in Pediatrics at Nagano Children’s Hospital and the University of Tokyo Hospital, as well as a Research Fellow at Kyoto University’s Graduate School of Medicine. After receiving primary and secondary education in the United Kingdom and the United States, I received my M.D. from the University of Tokyo in 2019. I am interested in the statistical modeling of gene regulatory networks for understanding human health and disease.
